# Formatting and layout (English résumé)

Rule set for English-language résumés aimed at US/Canada-style hiring. Summarised in our own
words from the r/EngineeringResumes wiki; the wiki itself stresses these are strong defaults,
not laws. For German CVs, `german-lebenslauf.md` overrides the points marked **[DE differs]**.

## Why the rules exist

A reviewer skims a résumé in well under a minute, and an ATS has to parse it. Every rule below
serves one of three goals: less visual noise, more room for relevant content, no wall of text.
Put the strongest, most relevant material at the top and on the left, where eyes land first.

## Layout

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Single column | No sidebars, no two-column layouts. |
| No graphics | No icons, photos, skill bars, logos or charts. **[DE differs: photo optional]** |
| No extra indentation | Section content starts at the left margin; bullets already indent. |
| Bullets, not paragraphs | Everything under a role or project is a bullet list. |
| Clear section breaks | Visible heading + rule or spacing; consistent spacing between sections and between entries. |
| White space | Margins ≥ 0.4 in (≈ 1 cm), line spacing ≥ ~1.07. Never cram to fit. |
| Left-aligned text | Never justify — it creates uneven word gaps. |
| Dates right-aligned | Dates sit flush with the right margin; bullet text never runs further right than the dates. **[DE differs: tabular left date column is common]** |

## Typography

- Modern, readable font: Calibri, Carlito, Arial, Helvetica, Lato, Bitstream/XCharter.
- Body ≥ 10.5 pt, solid black. No grey text, no hairline-thin weights; must survive greyscale printing.
- Emphasis is rare and never stacked: bold, italics and ALL CAPS are each used sparingly and not combined.
- Do not bold keywords inside bullets. Avoid italics for body text.

## Length

- 1 page by default. Roughly 1 page per decade of relevant experience; a 2nd page is justified
  around senior/staff level (10+ years). Hard cap of 2 pages even for senior candidates.
  **[DE differs: 1–2 pages is normal]**
- If it doesn't fit, cut the least relevant content (it can live in a portfolio) — never shrink
  fonts or margins below the minimums above.

## Dates (English)

- Format: `Mar 2021 – Present` or `March 2021 – Present`. Pick one style and use it everywhere.
- Month abbreviations: Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, June, July, Aug, Sept, Oct, Nov, Dec — no trailing period.
- Use months *or* years consistently; never seasons or semesters ("Winter 2022"); no day of month.
- Full four-digit years (`2023`, not `'23`); no numeric months (`9/2013`). **[DE differs: `09/2013` is standard]**
- Ranges use an en dash with a space on each side: `Mar 2012 – Mar 2022` (not `-`, not "to").
- Ongoing: `Present` (not Current, Now, Ongoing).

## ATS

Simple single-column layout, real text (not text in images), standard section names, and
plain-text contact details are what make a résumé parse cleanly. Fancy templates are the most
common cause of parsing problems; "ATS keyword stuffing" is not a substitute for relevant content.

## Grammar and proofreading

- Spell out uncommon abbreviations on first use, lowercase unless a proper noun:
  "scanning tunneling microscope (STM)".
- Capitalise only proper nouns. Run a grammar check, not just a spell check.
- Typos read as lack of attention to detail — always proofread the final PDF.
