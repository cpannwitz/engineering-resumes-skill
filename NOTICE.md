## berlin-job-search
German application conventions are aligned with, and defer to,
[berlin-job-search](https://github.com/umutyesildal/berlin-job-search) by Umut Yesildal (MIT).
The Markdown draft templates follow the shape of its CV and Lebenslauf templates so drafts can
move between both skills.
